# Roadmap

Where Ableton DJ MCP is going. This is the big picture, not a schedule: each
horizon builds on the one before it. Near-term work is tracked in
[issue #344](https://github.com/gpulga/ableton-dj-mcp/issues/344).

## North star

A studio partner that can take a track from a feeling to a finished, club-ready
record with you, and play it out with you. It writes, listens, arranges and
mixes, and it knows your sound well enough to make you faster without making you
sound like anyone else.

## Horizon 1: Hands

_The AI can do anything you can do in Live._

Every part of a session within reach: notes, sounds, samples, automation,
arrangement, mixing, routing, recording. Every action is precise, reversible and
verified, so working with the AI feels like working with a fast, careful
assistant engineer.

- The whole arrangement editable: insert time, fades, groove, stretching clips,
  moving between session and timeline
- Your sample libraries searchable by sound, not only by file name
- Audio as material: slice, convert, resample, chop vocals
- Setup in one line, working in minutes

## Horizon 2: Ears

_The AI hears what it makes._

An AI that only writes notes is working blind. This horizon gives it ears: it
listens to the mix, to a single track, to a sample, and checks its own work the
way a producer does.

- Loudness, spectrum, dynamics and stereo image of the mix, on demand
- Before/after comparisons, so every change is confirmed by listening
- Masking and frequency clashes spotted between parts
- Analysis of samples and stems: key, tempo, energy, structure

## Horizon 3: Taste

_The AI knows your sound._

Every producer has a signature. The tool learns yours, from your sessions and
your references, and brings it back to every new track.

- Reference tracks as targets: match the energy curve, the low end, the space
- A memory of your decisions: the sounds you reach for, the moves you reject
- Genre and arrangement knowledge that goes deep, not wide
- Ideas that sound like you on a good day, not like a template

## Horizon 4: The whole record

_From first loop to master._

Not just parts of a track, but the full journey: sketch, arrangement, sound
design, mix and a master that's ready to play.

- Arrangements built around the story of a track: tension, release, the drop
- Mixing moves with a reason behind them: gain staging, EQ, compression, space
- Versions on demand: club mix, radio edit, DJ-friendly intro and outro
- Exports ready for the booth

## Horizon 5: The booth

_Music played live, together._

The moment the music exists for: the dancefloor. The partner moves from the
studio to the set.

- Sets prepared from your library: harmonic and energy-aware track order
- Transitions, edits and blends built for your specific set
- Live performance support: loops, effects and scenes driven by intent
- Many people, one sound: collaboration across artists, sessions and stages

## How we get there

- **Less is more.** Each horizon ships as a few things that work every time.
- **Tested in Live.** Nothing ships on mocks alone.
- **Built in the open.** Ideas and feedback in
  [Discussions](https://github.com/gpulga/ableton-dj-mcp/discussions).

The [Vision](Vision.md) is why we're doing this.
