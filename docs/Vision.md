# Vision

> **The supreme form of communication is music.**
>
> The final evolution of human communication is music. Music doesn't reach you
> through words alone. It speaks soul to soul, thought by thought, synapse by
> synapse.
>
> It is the most complete way to share something with someone: two people inside
> the same sound, at the same moment.
>
> — Gabriel Pulga, creator of Ableton DJ MCP

That is why this exists. A dancefloor is the clearest example we know: people
who have never met, moving to the same kick, feeling the same breakdown, waiting
for the same drop. Electronic music is built for that moment, and the people who
make it deserve tools that keep them in it.

## What we're building

An AI studio partner for DJs and electronic music producers, working inside the
studio they already use. You describe the idea in words. The AI reads the
session, writes the notes, shapes the sound, arranges the sections and checks
its own work, while you stay in the creative seat.

## Principles

- **The artist leads.** The AI drafts, edits and suggests. You decide what the
  track says. Every change is visible, and every change can be undone.
- **Built for electronic music.** Grooves, basslines, builds, breakdowns,
  sweeps, phrasing: the vocabulary DJs and producers actually use, not generic
  "music".
- **Your sounds first.** Your samples, your loops, your instruments. The tool
  works with what you already own and love.
- **Less is more.** A focused set of tools that work every time beats a long
  list of features that half work.
- **It should listen.** Writing notes isn't enough. A real partner hears the
  music and checks its own changes, the way a producer does.

## Your sound

Taste is personal. There is no default sound here: the tool adapts to yours.
Tell it the artists and records you love, and it uses them as the reference for
grooves, pacing and sound, so what you make together still sounds like you.
