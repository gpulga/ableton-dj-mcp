# Vision

> **The supreme form of communication is music.**
>
> The final evolution of human communication is music. Music doesn't reach you
> through words alone. It speaks soul to soul, thought by thought, synapse by
> synapse.
>
> It is the most complete way to share something with someone: two people inside
> the same sound, at the same moment.
>
> Or more than two. Many, in tune and in union.
>
> — Gabriel Pulga, creator of Ableton DJ MCP

That is why this exists. A dancefloor is the clearest example we know: hundreds
of people who have never met, moving to the same kick, feeling the same
breakdown, waiting for the same drop. Electronic music is built for that moment,
and the people who make it deserve tools that keep them in it.

## What we're building

An AI studio partner for DJs and electronic music producers, working directly
inside Ableton Live. You describe the idea in words. The AI reads your set,
writes the notes, shapes the sound, arranges the sections and checks its own
work, while you stay in the creative seat.

## Principles

- **The artist leads.** The AI drafts, edits and suggests. You decide what the
  track says. Nothing changes in your set without a tool call you can see, and
  every action undoes in one step.
- **Built for electronic music.** House, tech house, indie dance, melodic and
  progressive, techno. Grooves, basslines, builds, breakdowns, filter sweeps,
  8-bar phrasing: the vocabulary DJs and producers actually use, not generic
  "music".
- **Your sounds first.** Your sample libraries, your loops, your racks. The tool
  searches, loads and reworks what you already own.
- **Less is more.** A focused set of tools that work every time beats a long
  list of features that half work. Every change is tested in Live before it
  ships.
- **Made for coding agents.** Claude Code is the home base: one line to install,
  one prompt to start. Any MCP client works, but the experience is tuned for an
  agent that can read, act and verify.
- **It should listen.** Writing notes isn't enough. The next step is an AI that
  hears the mix and checks its own changes, the way a producer does
  ([roadmap](https://github.com/gpulga/ableton-dj-mcp/issues/344)).

## The sound we build toward

Hypnotic, patient, melodic dance music: rolling basslines, long builds, reveals
that take eight bars instead of four, and grooves that are simple and still hit.
Artists like Dixon, Guy J and Adam Ten shaped what this tool is tuned for.
